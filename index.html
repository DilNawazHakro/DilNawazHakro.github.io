About

Educator and researcher in artificial intelligence, language technology, networks and technology adoption.

I have more than 24 years of experience in computer science, specialising in artificial intelligence, machine learning and cloud technologies, with a current focus on emerging technologies such as 5G and 6G. My doctoral work developed segmentation and feature-extraction methods for Sindhi optical character recognition, including a generalised segmentation algorithm for images of 84 languages in MATLAB.

I have supervised 39 PhD and MPhil students and authored 89 papers in national and international journals and conferences. I am committed to connecting students with research opportunities and to student-centred teaching.

Research

Core themes, drawn from publications and supervised theses.

Language technology

Sindhi and Urdu OCR

Segmentation, thinning, feature extraction and text-image databases for Sindhi, Urdu, Balochi and other Arabic-script languages.

Language technology

Speech, NER and retrieval

Sindhi speech recognition, named entity recognition for Sindhi and Urdu, information retrieval, Romanized Sindhi processing and sentiment analysis.

Networks

WSN, 5G and 6G

Genetic-algorithm optimisation in wireless sensor networks, vehicle-to-vehicle communication, 5G network analytics and e-health.

Security and cloud

Intrusion detection and cloud

IoT intrusion detection, serverless cloud-edge computing, blockchain supply chains, ransomware and cyber resilience.

Adoption

Islamic Fintech and e-Government

Islamic Fintech adoption readiness in Pakistan, e-government adoption, mobile healthcare adoption and AI-based eGuide for Oman's healthcare centres.

Inclusive technology

Serious games and learning

Game design and augmented-reality learning for children with dyslexia and autism; learning analytics and explainable AI for student performance.
